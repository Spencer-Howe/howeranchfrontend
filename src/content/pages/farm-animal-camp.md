# Farmer for a Day Experience

</div>

<div class="center">

Currently not offered, but we will post here if the program returns! We are currently offering [Open Farm Days and private tours by appointment only](https://thehoweranchregistration.com/).

Meet our gentle micro and mini cows, alpacas, donkeys, quirky goats, bunnies, ducks, and chickens, who are an uber social and lovable bunch!

When offered, the overnight farm animal camp is only for single family camping at Howe Ranch (no gatherings) with a cap of three families, but you can book a gathering/event for your entire group directly through our website.

Scroll down to learn more about the overnight farm camp!
