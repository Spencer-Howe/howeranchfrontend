Programming at our previous farm location Wildomar closed in June, and we’ve brought the Howe Ranch experience you know and love to our extraordinary new Montana home — a fairytale alpine village in the foothills of Glacier National in the fabled community of Mountain Brook on the east end of Kalispell, with sweeping valley and mountain views and a roaring glacier-fed creek winding through the property. Howe Ranch is a magical place to slow down, wander, celebrate and spend time with the sweetest little micro-Highland cows & barn friends imaginable. From [private animal experiences](https://thehoweranchregistration.com/) and [restorative getaways](https://thehoweranchregistration.com/) to [storybook Montana destination weddings](/weddings), milestone celebrations and [boutique seasonal events](https://thehoweranchregistration.com/), everything here is designed around true old-world hospitality.

And, from November 1 through January 31, a limited number of [Christmas Inn at Howe Ranch](https://thehoweranchregistration.com/) stays are available during our most magical season of the year — [Winter Wonderland](https://thehoweranchregistration.com/winter-wonderland). Reserve your [Winter Wonderland stay ](https://thehoweranchregistration.com/christmas-inn)now. Inside the Luxury Farm Lifestyle:

<div class="center">

Escape to Howe Ranch for a magical get away at this private animal farm nestled in the foothills of the Rocky Mountains. Experience farm life with boutique [Farm Animal Encounters](https://thehoweranchregistration.com), plan a Rustic Gathering with stunning city and mountain views, a [dream Montana wedding](/weddings), or a unique escape from urban life. Our activities directly support the care of our specialty bred [miniature animals](/photos) and rescues. Learn more about our animals here: [Howe Ranch Herd](/animals). Howe Ranch is located in the foothills of Glacier National Park in Kalispell, MT. The day visit experiences offered at the farm are wheelchair accessible (overnight accommodations are on the second floor), and we operate rain, snow or shine. Experiences will only be rescheduled if there is a safety concern (i.e. earthquake, flooding, unsafe wind) affecting the farm. For details reference our [Weather Polic](/_files/ugd/fca21d_8ac7b55be446452b9a4d5fe50bf7dfda.pdf)y.

Baby Moo Magic… where love, wonder, and sweet moments come to life.

</div>

Each fall and winter, Howe Ranch transforms into a lush, festive farm escape with enchanting details, soft lights, and unforgettable encounters with our beloved Mini Moos and farm friends. From lovely photo sessions to thoughtfully curated experiences, every farm visit is designed to celebrate connection, joy, and a little bit of magic in our peaceful Southern California setting, framed by panoramic city lights and mountain views.

✨ Step into Mini Moo Magic and pick what is right for you: 

Cuddle fluffy baby Highland calves, share kisses with the Mini Moos, meet fluffy Valais lambs and gentle donkeys, sip seasonal treats, and wander through romantic, whimsical seasonal scenes — all in a calm, boutique environment without crowds or chaos. Every detail is intentionally designed for comfort, togetherness, and meaningful memory-making.

Explore how you can experience Mini Moo Magic — whether you’re planning a private gathering with the baby cows, gifting a loved one a one-of-a-kind farm encounter with professional photos, or joining one of our limited Private Mini Moo experiences at the ranch.

Most photos below are of our beloved Howe Ranch in a small town in California called Wildomar. We cherish the 5 years we called this beautiful place home and are excited to continue the Howe Ranch Story in our magical storybook farm in Kalispell, MT. ❤️
