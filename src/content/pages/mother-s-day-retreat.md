# [Mother's Day Retreat at the Howe Ranch](https://thehoweranchregistration.com/)

</div>

Escape to the farm for a soul-soothing Mother's Day retreat featuring mini cow cuddles, sketching sessions with our donkeys, alpacas, and Highland calves, hands-on feeding with our fluffiest steers, and mindfulness exercises designed to de-stress and delight. 🫖 Top it off with sweet treats and artisan drinks and from the farm in a storybook setting with panoramic city and mountain views. You are welcome to bring your swimsuit for poolside relaxation. The retreat is most suitable for adults, but children who can modulate their behavior can attend, too. All guests 1 year and up need to be registered passholders. Passes are $30 per guest - Must [REGISTER](https://thehoweranchregistration.com/) prior to arrival.

Retreat Information:

We are ready to celebrate Mother's Day at Howe Ranch with a peaceful afternoon with you, surrounded by nature and the friendly mini cows, goats, donkeys, chickens, bunnies, and ducks. This retreat is for everyone who needs a little break and animal cuddles. Please note that since we will be spending time in animal enclosures/pasture, you need to acknowledge the [Safety and Inclusion Message](/_files/ugd/fca21d_e1b9412594904827a68686af6f09a554.pdf) prior to booking.

Explore tranquility and creativity at our unique wellness stations set around the farm! Each space pairs soothing animal interactions with mindful practices. Join us for a unique experience that combines the joy of animal companionship with mindful practices aimed at rejuvenating your spirit and releasing stress.

Treats, Splash, and Souvenirs

We'll be serving a variety of refreshments to keep you delighted. It’s our way of adding a little extra sweetness to your day of serene escape.

You are welcome to bring your swimsuit for poo time!

Before you leave you can built your own little mini souvenir bag.

Reflection

At the conclusion of our retreat, we'll gather in the tranquil setting of our cabanas. Here, you'll have the opportunity to dip your feet in the pool if the day is war and join in a reflective session with fellow attendees. This closing moment is designed for sharing insights and connecting over the day's experiences, allowing each of us to draw closure from our shared journey in a serene environment.

<div class="center">

All activities at Howe Ranch support the care of the animals, of which most are rescues from sanctuaries (501C) and farms that could no longer provide for them.

Tea and cupcakes served.

Spaces are limited, so [secure your session today](https://thehoweranchregistration.com/)!

Mom’s Moo Time is from 2:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.

on May 11.

[Reserve Now](https://thehoweranchregistration.com/)

</div>
