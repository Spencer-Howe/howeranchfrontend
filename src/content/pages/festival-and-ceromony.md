# Festival and Ceremony Area Photos

This venue has hosted ceremonies, live music, the fall festival and annual Christmas market, baby showers, Summer Fest, the petting zoo, and team building parties with stunning views of Temecula Valley and Palomar Mountain. Adjacent is a large, concrete multipurpose venue that can accommodate banquets and a dance area for up to 150 guests. Enjoy stunning views from this area, which also features a multi-use concession stand and two natural ceremony arches and backdrops. Guests arrive via a meandering path walking through a carefully curated garden, adorned with blooming flowers, lush foliage, and mature trees. The gentle whispers of a fountain accompany your footsteps, creating a soothing melody that harmonizes with the soft rustling of leaves.
