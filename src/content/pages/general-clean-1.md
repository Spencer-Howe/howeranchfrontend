# Valley View Patio Photos

A popular spot for sunset receptions, this venue offers panoramic views of Southern California mountains from Los Angeles, to San Bernardino (Big Bear), Mount San Jacinto (Palm Springs), Palomar Mountain ( San Diego) and the Murrieta and Temecula valley. Permanently furnished with rustic wine barrels, ideal for buffets, and offering multiple power outlets.
