FAQs

</div>

QUESTION: We'll have about 15 kids for my two-year old's birthday party. Do we have to pay for the kids? They don't eat much.

ANSWER: Yes, the per person event fee is $20 or $23 depending on your catering option, including chaperones, parents, friends and other guests in your party. Kids under 1 are free. The per person pass for guests over 1 year of age covers the significant costs associated with our public facing farm operations, including staffing and all the items offered in the experience.

QUESTION: I want to stop by in the morning to drop off coolers and put drinks on ice for our party at 2 p.m. Is that okay?

ANSWER: To maintain quality and safety standards, setup and drop-offs are limited to your three-hour rental. Additional setup time may be booked for $125/hour if needed.

QUESTION: We're having such a great time that we would like to extend our event by two hours. Can we do that?

ANSWER: In some cases, yes! If the venue and staff are available, and it would conclude by curfew time (8:00 PM), you can book additional time, up to four hours. You can use the hourly price sheet on our Events page to estimate the cost of this add-on. Additional time has to be booked and paid for prior to event wrap up.

### Farm Animal Encounter

</div>

<div class="center">

Each guest can interact with our farm animals, and responsible children can even feed and groom some of them! Learn about and interact with friendly farm animals, including several micro and mini-Highland cows, alpacas, adorable goats, Baroque donkeys, chickens and ducks.

</div>

<div class="center">

### Shaded Party Venue

</div>

<div class="center">

The Sand Pit by the Animal Barn offers a large farm event area with a pavilion, and picnic table and benches seating for your party. The sand pit offers an array of toys for all ages. You also have the option to utilize the games available on site, such as lasso and cornhole. Enjoy seasonal photo fun with the animal barn backdrop.

</div>

<div class="center">

### Party Catering Included

</div>

<div class="center">

Included in your Petting Zoo Party package is a choice of Pizza or Tacos (+$3/Guest, min. 15) for everyone provided by preferred local vendors. Plates and napkins included. Bring your own drinks and decor. If you want to cater your gathering yourself, we can deduct $2/per person (not the base venue fee).
