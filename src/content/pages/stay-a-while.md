# Stay Over!

The Bed & Breakfast for Family & Friends is open! Experience the calming effects of country living. Each bedroom is lovingly decorated with just enough farm charm. All bedrooms and bathrooms have been recently remodeled and professionally decorated. Two bedrooms are currently available for travelers within our network only. At this time not available for members of the public or residents of the area.
